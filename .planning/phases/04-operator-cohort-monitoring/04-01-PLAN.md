## Phase Goal

Sourced from ROADMAP.md Phase 4 (task-shaped goal; MVP user-story reframe available via `/gsd mvp-phase 4`):

> On the authenticated console, the operator monitors each advertised cohort's members, pending submissions, co-sign progress, and anchor status in real time, turning the read-only telemetry tab into the operator's live view of on-demand cohorts.

Derived slice framing (not authoritative): **As a** self-hosting service operator, **I want to** open a cohort and watch who has joined and how many seats remain, live, on my protected console, **so that** I can see at a glance what is happening without reading logs.

This plan is the phase TRACER: the thinnest end-to-end monitoring path (runner events -> server fold -> gated detail read -> polled console drill-down), proving the whole polled-snapshot architecture (D-19) on one honest fact (members + seats) before any expansion. It is additive and production-quality: the dashboard-SSE retirement (D-02/D-19) and all deeper drill-down surfaces land in later plans on top of this proven seam.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
