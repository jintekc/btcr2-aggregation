## Gap source

This plan closes **audit defect 2** from `.planning/phases/05-operator-cohort-lifecycle-control/05-AUDIT.md` (entry 2, HIGH, real funds), also filed as `.planning/todos/pending/2026-07-29-stand-down-the-funding-watch-under-the-broadcast-kill-switch.md`.

Both skeptics reproduced it end to end against the shipped code with a stub esplora: the cohort correctly stood down (the service logged that it was not publishing its beacon transaction, and zero broadcasts were sent) while the stub esplora was still polled, and both the anonymous `GET /v1/funding/:id` and the gated drill-down kept reporting `state: waiting` with `suggestedMinSats: 2000`, before AND after the cohort ended.

**Read the skeptics, not only the finder.** Three corrections change the scope of the fix:

1. A second consequence the finder missed, on the participant side and reachable anonymously: `publicFunding` returning true latches `liveCohort: true` in `packages/web/src/stores/participant.ts`, so `CohortPage.tsx` tells every seated participant of a stood-down cohort that the cohort anchors on-chain and that it is waiting for the operator to fund its beacon address. Both are false. **A fix that does not close this leaves the honesty defect alive on the participant side.** This plan closes it at the source rather than in the participant store: with no funding view recorded, `publicFunding` returns false and the latch never arms. No web-side change is needed, and Task 2 asserts that through the served value rather than assuming it.
2. "Forever" is bounded, not literal: the monitor's `fundingViews` map holds at most 24 entries with oldest-first eviction, and `dismissEnded` deletes the entry. So the stale prompt persists until the operator dismisses the record, 24 further cohorts evict it, or the process restarts. Long-lived, not permanent. The plan does not claim more than that.
3. The `Needs funding` summary chip only appears while the cohort is still in the live session; once it settles the row reverts to its ended chip. What is durable is the retained funding view driving the drill-down card and the anonymous read.

**What the shipped SUMMARY claims, and why the executor must not trust it here.** `05-08-SUMMARY.md` states that a stood-down cohort "never waits for funding, never reads a UTXO". That is true of the AUTHORITATIVE wait in `packages/service/src/tx.ts` and false of the DISPLAY watch in `packages/service/src/funding-watch.ts`, which is the one that drives every funding surface the operator and participants actually see. The same claim appears in the `onProvideTxData` comment block in `packages/service/src/index.ts` ("it never waits for funding, never reads a UTXO"): that comment is scoped to the tx-data builder and is accurate there, so leave it alone and do not widen it.

<objective>
Make the broadcast kill switch stand down the funding surface too, so the console cannot ask the operator to send real bitcoin to a beacon address the fixture path will never read or spend.

Purpose: the kill switch already gets the two money legs right. It picks the fixture tx builder and it refuses to publish. The gap is the DISPLAY watch, which is wired unconditionally at `keygen-complete` and whose very first poll records a `waiting` funding view. That view is what draws the copyable `bitcoin:` URI, the "send at least N sats in one single payment" instruction, and, at the default throwaway recovery key, the line stating that funds sent there are unrecoverable. Nothing on the per-cohort surface distinguishes a cohort advertised before the switch (which genuinely must be funded) from one advertised after it (which must not), which is what makes acting on the prompt plausible rather than absurd.

Output: one guard at the watch's start using the predicate that already exists, the monitor exposed for assertion, a spec that fails against the current code, and an honest, pinned, three-places disclosure of the one health bit the guard stops feeding.
</objective>
